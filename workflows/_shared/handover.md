# Handover format (DRAFT)

Every step ends with a handover in exactly this shape. Hard limits in brackets.
Omit nothing; write "none" if empty. No narration of what you did step by step.

- **Goal** [1–2 sentences]: what this step set out to do.
- **Decisions** [≤ 7 bullets]: each decision + the reason.
- **Rejected** [≤ 5 bullets]: alternatives considered and why not.
- **Files touched** [list]: paths, one line each on why.
- **Verify** [≤ 5 steps]: how the next person/agent confirms this works.
- **Open questions** [≤ 5 bullets]: what is unresolved and who should answer.
